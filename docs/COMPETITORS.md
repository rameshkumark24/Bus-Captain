> Researched **2026-11-23/24** for the product in [IDEA.md](IDEA.md). Risks and legal findings are in [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md).

## How to read this

**Labels:** every claim carries one of:
- **SEEN** — opened the page, or pulled the data myself;
- **REPORTED** — someone else says so;
- **ESTIMATED** — a tool's guess, or mine.

**Sources:** each claim also has a source ID (C01…). The table in §9 gives each source's link and the date it was seen. Review quotes link to the review itself.

**Method:**
- **Store data:** Google Play **India** pages, plus Google Play's public review feed (the same data the Play page loads). Star filter, newest first, India locale unless stated.
- **Counting people:** Google Play allows one review per account per app, so **a count of reviews = a count of separate people**. Theme counts are keyword-based. A review that mentions two themes counts in both.
- **Other tools:** Google Trends (web search, India, past 5 years), Apple's public App Store lookup, and the Wayback Machine.

**Not verified** (would strengthen this — see §10):
- YouTube, Instagram, Reddit and Telegram community sizes. The parallel researchers hit the usage limit twice before finishing their web parts.
- Revenue estimates. AppMagic needs a login; Statista is paywalled.

- **Indian bus games are a mass market:**

## 4. For each top complaint: why has it not been fixed?

This section is my judgement, from the evidence above.

1. **Ads that interrupt (Terminal Master especially) — DELIBERATE.**
   - Ads *are* the business. Terminal Master sells remove-ads at ~$9.99, yet players who paid "still need to see some ads to go forward". Progress-gating rewarded ads are part of the design.
   - The developer answered **none of 159** ad complaints.
   - Fixing it breaks their revenue. For us it's an opening at a cost: v1 has no ads; v1.1 is rewarded-first and 18+ only (P-5). We'll earn less per player; that's the trade.
2. **"Not my place / not authentic" (Bus Simulator India, Bus Sim Kerala) — SEGMENT, plus HARD *for them*.**
   - Maleo and Byteraft serve all of India (or Indonesia) with 3D maps. A state-accurate 3D map is expensive, and Tamil Nadu players are a slice of their customers.
   - Evidence: Coimbatore with Hindi shop signs; no update since 28 Apr 2026 despite constant map requests; 0 developer replies.
   - **For a 2D Tamil Nadu-only idle game, authenticity is cheap:** signs, place names, bus shapes, Tamil text. The real, structural gap is **SEGMENT, and our format makes it cheap.**
3. **Stuck progression and running out of content (idle tycoons) — HARD, partly DELIBERATE.**
   - Level-based idle games burn finite content. Several "stuck" complaints are task-tracking bugs.
   - Terminal Master *did* add content when asked (the train station reply). That suggests the problem is cost, not ignorance.
   - **It will be just as hard for us.** Prestige and the simulator targets help, but content is still finite. **I cannot tell how much of "stuck" is bugs versus design.**
4. **Livery friction** (file juggling, broken downloads, "only who knows coding") — **for Bus Simulator Indonesia: CANNOT TELL.** Its livery system works for power users (templates edited outside the game); why Maleo hasn't built an in-game painter isn't visible. For the livery apps: likely low-investment, ad-funded apps [ESTIMATED], so the gap is real.
   - **But read this straight: what fans want painted is *real* operators and politics** — Komban, SRS, MTC, TNSTC, TVK (C18, C22, C24). **We legally can't offer those** (owner rules; RESEARCH.md L-14). Our painter removes the *friction*, not the *wish*. That part of the gap only looks real from outside.
5. **Bugs and freezes — CANNOT TELL** (it could be MISSED or HARD). It's universal, it affects us equally, and the quality bar in IDEA.md addresses it.

## 7. The parity trap — what day-one users expect just because everyone has it

| Expectation (everyone has it) | Must we? | Can we refuse and survive? |

## 8. The honest verdict

**Is there a market? YES** — for bus games in India, proven many times over:
- Bus Simulator Indonesia 10Cr+;
- three India-themed sims at 1Cr+ each, one of them new in 2026;
- an idle bus tycoon at 1Cr+;
- hundreds of new India reviews every few days.

People use these every week and pay through ads and in-app purchases. How much they pay is not verified.

**Is there a gap? A narrow but real one — this is a well-served market with a thin angle.**
- **Real:**
  1. **Nobody serves Tamil Nadu authentically.** Bus Simulator India's Coimbatore has Hindi shop signs, and Tamil Nadu asks recur in 2026 reviews.
  2. **The idle bus tycoon leader's players are drowning in ads** (51% of its 3-star reviews).
  3. **Making a bus "yours" is painful today** (file juggling, broken mod apps).
- **Thinner than it looks from outside:**
  1. Explicit Tamil Nadu asks are about **1%** of review text.
  2. Fans want **real** operators and politics painted, which we can't legally offer.
  3. Indian bus fans search for **simulators**, not idle games ("idle/tycoon game" Trends 1–2 vs 61).
  4. Livery interest is **falling** (−70% since 2022).

**What this means for the plan:**
- **The biggest risk is format fit, not demand.** Will a Tamil Nadu bus fan who loves driving sims accept a tycoon? v1 is the right cheap test of that.
- The evidence points to distribution as the killer. The share card and the Tamil Nadu-authentic look are the two things no competitor has. They should be **the** priorities of v1's polish, ahead of extra content.
- **One opening to watch:** a Kerala sim player asked for "ksrtc vs pvt bus competition". The *management* fantasy (running a private bus company against the government buses) exists among sim players. That's our core loop, but the evidence is one review [SEEN]; its weight is ESTIMATED.

### 2. The one unique selling point

**What it's built from:**
- By raw count, the top unfixed complaint is **ads** (Terminal Master: 51% of its 3-star reviews). But that audience is global — its reviewers price things in $ and £ [SEEN C18] — and the ads are deliberate (§4.1).
- **Among Indian bus-game players — our users — the top unfixed complaint is "it isn't my place":**
  - Bus Simulator India: 64 of 150 complain about maps, plus 22 about regional authenticity;
  - Bus Sim Kerala: 46 + 22 of 224 [SEEN C18].
- That complaint stays unfixed because of who the competitors serve (§4.2), and it's cheap to fix in a 2D game set only in Tamil Nadu. It's the foundation.

**The sentence a player would repeat to a friend:**

> **"It's a bus company game that's actually Tamil Nadu — in Tamil, with real towns like Kovai and Ooty, and you paint your own buses."**

This wording gets tested in the closed test (U-6).

**Class: AIMED.**
- The same kind of game — an idle bus tycoon with customisation — for a group nobody serves well: Tamil Nadu bus fans, in Tamil.
- **Not NEW:** idle bus tycoons exist [SEEN C01, C02], and Bus Simulator India even has a Coimbatore area [SEEN C18].
- **Not measurably BETTER on the loop itself.** "Better" applies only to livery making (zero files vs folder errors and failed downloads, above).

**Would players notice?** Yes. They spot place details at once:
- the Coimbatore Hindi-signs review;
- "I see only one kind of bus in traffic" ([review](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.maleo.bussimulatorin&reviewId=bb5a1499-a7db-4a33-ae9e-43cca5e1a765));
- the Kerala-pride 5★ review [SEEN C18].

Tamil boards and Tamil Nadu towns appear in our first 10 seconds (§4 below).

**Would they switch?** Honestly, **mostly they would add us, not switch:**
- Sim fans keep their driving sims, because we're a different genre. Regional players already run two bus games side by side: one Bus Sim Kerala player compares it with Bus Simulator Indonesia — "…i will play the bus simulator indonatioa but this kerala bus one trearing soo smo…" (21 Jan 2026, [review](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.GamerChunkZ.BusSimulatorKerala&reviewId=c369634e-67ad-4b38-b415-f352d46afecb)) [SEEN C18].

- **Still unproven: that Tamil Nadu sim fans enjoy a tycoon at all.** Explicit Tamil Nadu requests are about 1% of review text, and Indians search "bus simulator" (61) far more than "idle/tycoon game" (1–2) [SEEN C18, C22]. v1 exists to test exactly this.
